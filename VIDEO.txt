WALKTHROUGH VIDEO
=================

Video URL: https://youtu.be/qyioRI18x8U

A 4:47 walkthrough of every feature, with subtitle captions explaining each
step as it happens. Chapters and the full description are in
docs/VIDEO_DESCRIPTION.md; the narration script is in
docs/WALKTHROUGH_SCRIPT.md.

Covered, in order: sign-in, the owned/shared document list, search, document
creation, inline rename, autosave states, rich-text formatting, the link
dialog, undo history and word count, file import (append/replace and
create-new), an unsupported-file rejection, presence with a second user, a
real concurrent-edit conflict and its recovery through version history,
sharing roles, dark mode, and read-only enforcement for a viewer.
